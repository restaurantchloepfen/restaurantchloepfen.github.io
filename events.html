Events

Celebrate with us

Directly next to our Restaurant you find our Barn which is ideal for events.

Our Barn has a fascinating past. Originally located at Eschenbach Monastery it served many purposed over the years, including as a cheese cellar.
In 2026, the barn underwent an extensive renovation, which transformed the barn into a modern event venue full of character and charme.
Today, our Barn provides the perfect setting for various events.
