Our history

1796

The beginning

The original buidling of the Wirtshaus Chlöpfe, know among the locals as "d'Chlöpfe", dates back until 1796
Thanks to an old wine-serving license, Chlöpfen has a long history of hospitality even before then

1968/69

Second stop

Since 1968/69 the property belongs to the Burger family.
Originally the intended for a large storage and operational project, it remained a restaurant due to changing business needs.

2026

Fourth stop

As the last tenancy agreement ended in eraly 2026, the restaurant remained closed

2027

Today

On the 7th of April 2027, Chlöpfen will reopen its doors.
Operating under a new concept developed by a group of students of the SHL Swiss Hospitality Management School Lucerne, Chlöpfen writes its next chapter.
The spirit remains the same, but with a new menu and team, we are excited for what is next.
